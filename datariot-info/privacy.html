Privacy Policy

How Datariot handles your account, clips, messages and Orvelis AI requests across our websites and apps.

What we collect

Account and profile
Your email, username, profile details and the information needed to sign you in. If you sign in with a third-party account, we receive the account details that provider shares for sign-in.
Content and communication
Videos and audio you upload, captions, comments, video replies, messages you send and information you give us when you report a problem or contact support.
Activity and device information
Interactions such as views, likes and saves, plus technical information such as your IP address, browser, device type and error logs.

How we use your data

We use this information to run your account, publish and deliver clips, enable conversations, and recommend content based on your activity. We also use it to investigate reports, prevent abuse, resolve technical issues and respond to support requests.

We use browser storage and similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as your theme. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

What other people can see

Your public profile and public posts, including their captions and public replies, can be seen by other people. Public clips may be accessible outside the app through a shared link. Direct messages are shown to the people in that conversation.

People may save or share copies of content you make public. Deleting a post on Datariot does not remove copies that other people already made.

How Orvelis AI handles data

When you use Orvelis, we process your question and any recent messages needed to answer it. For supported public clips, Orvelis can analyze the video and audio along with the title and description. It can also reuse a saved analysis for that clip.

Google’s Gemini processes supported public clips. General chat requests may be sent to Google or OpenAI to generate a response. When you ask Orvelis to check a claim, the feature may also use search. Leave passwords, private messages and other sensitive details out of prompts.

Who receives data

We use Vercel to host our websites and Supabase for services such as authentication, databases and media storage. Our AI providers process the information described above to deliver Orvelis. Depending on where you and our providers are located, some data may be processed in other countries.

We may disclose information when required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep data

We keep account data while your account is active and retain other records as long as needed for the purposes described here. Some records may need to be kept after an account closes, such as records needed to meet legal obligations, handle disputes or prevent abuse.

How to access, correct or delete data

You can edit profile details in Datariot. To request a copy of your data, correct information you cannot edit or ask us to delete your account and associated data, contact privacy@datariot.xyz. Tell us what you want to change and, if possible, write from the email associated with your account. We may ask you to verify that the account is yours.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, receive a portable copy of your data, withdraw consent where we rely on it, or complain to a data protection authority.

Age limits and policy updates

Datariot is for people aged 13 or older. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, contact privacy@datariot.xyz so we can review it.

We post updates here and change the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your personal data, we will provide a notice through the service or by email.
