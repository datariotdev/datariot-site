[ 01 — THE FEED ] SHORT-FORM VIDEO  //  EVERY CLIP HAS A POINT

Every clip
has a point.

Most feeds are built to keep you scrolling. This one is built so you walk away knowing something: a trick, a fact, a skill, an argument worth hearing, from a real person on camera.

  • How lidar sees the street — Lidar times laser pulses as they bounce back and turns the delays into a 3D map.
  • What an AI chatbot actually does — A chatbot predicts likely next words. Facts still need checking.
  • Should we prioritize logic over emotion? — Emotion tells you what matters; logic tells you what to do about it.
  • Polar bears aren’t actually white — Their fur is translucent and hollow; it only looks white. The skin underneath is black.
  • Compound interest, in one chart — Interest earns interest, so time does the heavy lifting: $100 at 7% is $761 after 30 years.
  • Why tall towers sway on purpose — Tall towers are designed to sway a little; flexing absorbs wind that a rigid tower would fight.
  • The 20-20-20 rule for screen eyes — Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

  1. Learn something in a minute

    Clips are short and get to it. No ten-minute intros, no “wait for it”.

  2. Keep the point

    Every clip has a Deep Dive one tap away: the summary, the key terms and where to learn more.

  3. Ask, or answer back

    Stuck? Ask Orvelis about the video. Disagree? Reply on camera, with your own face.

[ 08 — FAQ ]

Questions, answered.

The short version of what Datariot is, how it works and what happens to your clips. Something missing? Ask us on Discord.

01What is Datariot?

A short-video app where every clip has a point. People explain, show or argue something worth knowing, and anyone can answer back with a clip of their own.

02How is it different from TikTok or Reels?

It is built around what a clip says, not how long it keeps you watching. Every clip has a Deep Dive, replies are videos instead of comment wars, and Orvelis, our AI, checks the logic.

03What is Orvelis?

The AI inside Datariot. For every clip it builds a Deep Dive: a summary, three key terms and three places to learn more. It answers your questions about the video and flags fallacies and manipulation, timestamped to the second.

04What is a video reply?

Instead of typing a comment, you answer on camera with a clip of your own. Replies stack under the original, so the whole exchange stays watchable from start to finish.

05Who decides who made the better point?

Viewers vote, and Orvelis marks the logic. Votes come from verified people, so bots and sock puppets can’t swing them.

06Do I have to show my face?

To post, yes. Datariot is face-first: every clip is tied to a verified person on camera, which keeps anonymous pile-ons out.

07What can I post about?

Anything you can explain, show or argue: science, engineering, AI, logic, sport, gaming, lifestyle, politics, comedy and more. Keep it educational, informative or constructive, and bring evidence when you argue.

08When can I get it, and who can join?

Datariot is in beta. The iOS and Android apps are coming soon: join the waitlist and we’ll send your invite when it’s your turn. You need to be 13 or older.

09What happens to my data and my clips?

We don’t sell your personal data. You keep ownership of everything you post, and you can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. The details are in our Privacy Policy.
